# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thai patients and their families booking home-care and medical-transport
services through a mobile app. Often booking on behalf of an elderly
relative, sometimes under time pressure (a parent needs a ride to a
clinic today). Mobile-first, one-handed use, variable literacy with
digital forms — copy and flows must be unambiguous without hand-holding.

## Product Purpose

CareMate (customer app) lets a family book, pay for, and track home-care
and medical-transport services for a relative, and manage the people
(members) and addresses those bookings apply to. Success is a parent or
sibling being able to book a trustworthy caregiver for a relative in
under two minutes, and knowing exactly what's happening with that booking
at every step (matched, en route, arrived) without needing to call anyone.

## Brand Personality

A companion, not a clinic. Warm and human where the subject matter
(elderly care, medical transport) could easily read cold or bureaucratic.
Confident and calm under the pressure of a real booking, never playful at
the expense of trust.

Three words: **caring · clear · dependable**

Emotional goal: the person booking should feel like a capable friend is
handling the logistics, not like they're filling out a hospital intake
form.

## Anti-references

- Sterile hospital/insurance-portal aesthetic (cold blues, clinical
  iconography, zero warmth)
- Decorative glassmorphism / blur used as the default surface treatment
  with no structural reason — the previous "Aurora Glass" pass leaned on
  blurred hero blobs everywhere, which reads as templated AI output, not
  a considered system
- Generic SaaS scaffolding: identical card grids, gradient text, tiny
  tracked eyebrows above every section
- Copying the sibling **Partner app**'s palette outright — same family
  (soft color washes behind hero content, pill actions, warm depth via
  shadow not flat tint) but its own identity, not a reskin

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth earns trust here** — this app sits next to decisions about an
   elderly relative's care; every screen should feel considered, not
   transactional.
2. **One clear next action** — a person booking under time pressure
   should never have to search for the primary button.
3. **Status is never ambiguous** — booking/matching states use label +
   color + icon together, never color alone.
4. **Shared family, distinct identity with the Partner app** — both apps
   are recognizably "CareMate" (soft washes, pill controls, generous
   corner radii, warm-tinted depth) but the customer app's own hue
   (teal → blue, from the real app icon) must never be mistaken for the
   partner app's orange.
5. **Craft over decoration** — motion, blur, and color must justify
   themselves structurally; nothing ships "because it looks premium."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text/UI components). All Thai
copy — verify chosen typefaces actually cover Thai glyphs (a prior
typeface choice, Manrope, silently lacked Thai coverage; always confirm
in a Thai-rendered screenshot, not just the font name). Status conveyed
by label + color + icon, never color alone, for users with color vision
deficiency. Reduced motion respected for any added animation.
